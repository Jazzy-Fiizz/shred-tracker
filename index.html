For the liver

  • No added sugar or fructose. No soft drinks, no juice. Fructose is processed in the liver — the most direct driver of liver fat.
  • Cut refined carbs. Bread, pastry, crisps, biscuits.
  • Alcohol stays at zero. Already true — keep it.
  • Mediterranean base. Olive oil, oily fish, veg, pulses, nuts.
  • Creatine 5g daily, with food. Tell your GP: it raises serum creatinine, and ramipril means your kidneys get monitored.

Your gut

  • Eat slowly, chew properly, smaller meals.
  • Nothing within 3 hours of bed.
  • No NSAIDs — ibuprofen, naproxen.
  • Don't self-eliminate foods. EoE triggers are usually dairy, wheat, egg, soy, nuts or seafood — but guessing wrecks your protein target for no confirmed benefit. That's the dietician's job. Chase the referral.
